01 / WHO WE ARE

More than a service.
A place to grow.

Established in 2021 in Udma, Kasaragod, Live to Smile was founded on a simple vision: to create more smiles by helping people discover their potential and navigate life's challenges.

From counselling and motivation to learning support and educational pathways, we aim to make meaningful guidance more accessible.
