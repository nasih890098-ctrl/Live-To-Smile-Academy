Ahammed Shareen is a psychologist, trainer and mental health entrepreneur based in Kasaragod, Kerala. Before moving into the care and mental health field, he spent 15 years in the corporate sector, including work with Etisalat in the UAE.

Motivated by a longstanding interest in human psychology, he transitioned into mental health and counselling. His listed qualifications include a Master's degree in Psychology from Annamalai University, a Diploma in Community Mental Health from NIMHANS, and a certificate programme in the basics of addiction management through NIMHANS.

His earlier education also includes an MBA in Business Process Outsourcing from Sikkim Manipal University and a BBA from the University of Calicut.

As Co-Founder of Live to Smile, he contributes to the organisation's vision of making support, guidance and personal development more accessible to the community.
